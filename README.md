## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, deterministic) writes a single pitch sentence — no more than 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`: a startup idea so strange it sounds like a joke, yet specific enough that a reader can see the customers and the money.

The pitch: friendly strangers hired to wave from your driveway at passing neighbours each morning, sold to new homeowners who pay $99 a month to look like the most popular person on a street that never talks. Status signalling is a real market — rent-a-crowd, fake queues, bought followers — and the sentence names the odd thing being sold, who pays, and why it holds together.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
